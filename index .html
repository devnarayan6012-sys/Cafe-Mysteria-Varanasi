About Cafe Mysteria

A stylish escape where food meets play

Tucked away in the heart of Varanasi, Cafe Mysteria is a modern luxury lounge built for slow evenings and long conversations. We blend the warmth of a fine restaurant with the easy joy of game night — no rush, no noise, just good company.

Curated lighting, plush seating, a kitchen that takes dinner seriously, and a lounge of high-end PC rigs. Whether you're on a date, with friends, or flying solo, there's a table waiting for you.

  • ✓ Handcrafted food & signature drinks
  • ✓ 20+ high-performance PC rigs
  • ✓ Cosy, warm-lit ambience
  • ✓ Open till late, every day
